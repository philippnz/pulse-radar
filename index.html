Studio practice

Color, memory, and rooms that feel half awake

Mira Vale builds paintings through scraped layers, dry-brush marks, and blocks of saturated color. Familiar subjects become unstable: windows flatten into fields, vessels turn monumental, and gardens dissolve into weather.

The portfolio is organized like a working archive, giving each piece room to breathe while keeping dimensions, medium, series, and availability close at hand.
